Privacy policy

TriCarbon for iPhone and Ubuntu · effective 23 October 2026

Home · What's new · How to test · Support · Privacy

TriCarbon does not collect any personal data.

  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not send anything about you over the internet. Its only connection is a check, at most once a day, of the public list of the newest versions (App Store, www.trisport.me) so it can tell you when an update is available; it sends no personal data, identifiers or usage information. All emission factors and texts are included in the app.
  • Your events, inventory lines, aspects, settings and custom factors are stored only on your device (in the app's own storage, visible to you in the Files app).
  • When you export a report or event file, it is shared only where you choose to send it (for example Mail or Files).
  • Deleting the app deletes all its data from your device.

Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings. These do not identify you.

Politika privatnosti (crnogorski)

TriCarbon ne prikuplja nikakve lične podatke. Nema naloga, reklama, analitike ni praćenja. Aplikacija ništa o vama ne šalje preko interneta. Jedina veza je provjera, najviše jednom dnevno, javnog spiska najnovijih verzija (App Store, www.trisport.me) da bi vas obavijestila o novoj verziji; ne šalje lične podatke, identifikatore ni podatke o korišćenju. Vaši podaci ostaju samo na vašem uređaju i dijele se jedino kada ih sami izvezete. Brisanjem aplikacije brišu se i svi njeni podaci.
